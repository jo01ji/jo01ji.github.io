The Voron 0.2

The Voron 0.2 is the smallest printer in the open-source Voron family: an enclosed CoreXY with a 120 mm cube build volume. This page collects what is specific to it. The general physics (kinematics, ringing, flow, layer welding and mods) is in The FDM polymer 3D printer. This page will grow into a build and tuning log once a V0.2 is assembled.

1 · The machine

A small, enclosed CoreXY that is already where most bed-slinger mods try to go

  • Kinematics. CoreXY with a 120 × 120 × 120 mm build volume (ref. 1). The small frame is stiff and the moving mass is small, so its resonance frequencies are high; measure them with an accelerometer before chasing other upgrades (see section 4 of the FDM lesson).
  • Firmware. Klipper, so input shaping, pressure advance and macros are configuration changes, not firmware rebuilds.
  • Toolhead. The V0.2 is designed around the Mini Stealthburner with a BMG-geared direct-drive extruder (ref. 2). Its hotend and extruder set the flow limit of section 5 of the FDM lesson.
  • Enclosure. Already enclosed, so ABS and ASA print well. A carbon filter such as a Nevermore is the usual first addition, because ABS and ASA give off fumes and particles.
2 · First steps after the build

Calibrate before upgrading

  1. An accelerometer run (ADXL345 and Klipper's SHAPER_CALIBRATE) to set the input shaper.
  2. A pressure advance calibration.
  3. A max-flow test, to know the hotend's real volumetric limit.
  4. A chamber thermistor and a heat-soak macro, so prints start with the frame at temperature.
  5. A carbon filter for ABS and ASA.

Each step corresponds to a section of the FDM lesson, and all are cheap.

3 · Mods that suit a V0

From the general mod list, the ones that commonly fit a V0

ModWhy on a V0
Accelerometer for input shapingMeasures the resonance instead of guessing it from a test print
High-flow hotend or nozzleRaises the melt-rate limit; check it fits the Mini Stealthburner
Hardened or carbide-tipped nozzleFor carbon- and glass-filled filaments
PEI spring-steel sheetAdhesion when hot, easy release when cool
Carbon and HEPA filter (Nevermore)Fumes and particles from ABS and ASA in the enclosed chamber
Chamber thermistor and heat soakStable first layer and dimensions
Toolhead board (USB or CAN) with an umbilicalFewer flexing wires to break
Better part coolingSmall parts at speed are often cooling-limited
Filament runout sensor, nozzle wiper, camera and lightsReliability and remote checks

Fit varies between V0 revisions and toolheads; check each mod's own instructions against the build before ordering parts. A multi-material unit is possible, but the small bed limits the purge tower.

4 · In the lab

What a 120 mm cube is good for

The 120 mm cube is enough for sample holders, polishing jigs, mounts and the printed parts of the inverted microscope design. Print anything that sits near a hot stage or furnace in ASA or PC rather than PLA, and use the material table in the FDM lesson for the limits.
