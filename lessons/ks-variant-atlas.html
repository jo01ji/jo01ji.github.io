The 24 Kurdjumov–Sachs variants

{111}γ ∥ {011}α  ·  ⟨101⟩γ ∥ ⟨111⟩α

One austenite (fcc) grain can transform into 24 martensite (bcc) orientations that all satisfy the same KS relationship. Each one puts a close-packed {011}α plane on one of the four {111}γ planes and lines up a close-packed ⟨111⟩α row with a ⟨110⟩γ row inside it. Pick a variant anywhere on the page and every view follows.

Misorientation is the smallest rotation between two variants after applying the 24 cubic symmetry operators. Inside one plane group: 60° (twin-related pairs such as V1/V2), 49.47°, and the 10.53° sub-block partner (V1/V4).

Packets

The six variants sharing one {111}γ plane form a packet in lath martensite. They are the same habit plane, rotated in-plane by the three ⟨110⟩γ rows.

Blocks and sub-blocks

Within a packet, pairs 10.53° apart (V1/V4, V2/V5, V3/V6) share a Bain axis and form a block of two sub-blocks.

Bain groups

Every variant is close to one of the three Bain orientations, compressed along [100]γ, [010]γ or [001]γ. Each Bain group holds 8 variants, two from each plane.
