What the project is asking

Three open questions in the austenite→ferrite transformation that the prototype organises evidence around. Plain language first; expand for the exact technical framing used in the data.

Inspect the evidence

A small curated set of source-grounded propositions. Each card carries its paper, source location, role (observation / interpretation / proposed inference), review status and source-version id. A verified quotation is shown; its scientific interpretation is not yet reviewed.

Explore a historical cutoff

Choose a cutoff year. The prototype shows which supplied evidence would have been available by then and why the rest is excluded. These are precomputed eligibility bundles, not a reconstructed knowledge state — no intermediate history is invented.

Arguments & discriminators

Phase 4 adds a typed argument layer over the same evidence: named candidate mechanisms for each case, formal discriminators (what an observation can and — just as important — cannot separate), and relation types that keep "mentions" distinct from "support." Built offline; every record is machine-draft or source-text-checked, none expert-reviewed.

Candidate mechanisms

The competing explanations for each question, with their own ids so a named alternative is representable rather than forced into a global vocabulary.

Discriminators — what an observation can and cannot separate

For each proposed observable: what each candidate would show, and — the scientifically careful part — what the observation does not distinguish and where it stays inconclusive.
