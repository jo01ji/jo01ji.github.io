2 · Reading it

What the surfaces are

Ae3

The temperature where ferrite first appears on slow cooling. Austenite stabilisers such as Mn, Ni, Cu and Co push it down, and ferrite stabilisers such as Si, Cr, Mo, V, Al and W push it up. On the hypereutectoid side it becomes the line where ferrite joins austenite and carbide.

Acm and upper Ae1

The temperature where the first carbide appears. Below the eutectoid carbon it is the upper Ae1, the top of the three-phase band. Above it, it is Acm, the cementite solubility line.

Lower Ae1

The temperature where the last austenite disappears. In plain Fe–C the two Ae1 temperatures are one line at 726.5 °C. A third element opens them into a band where ferrite, austenite and carbide coexist.
